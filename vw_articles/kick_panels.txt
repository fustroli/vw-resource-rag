Kick Panels
Rear Kick Panels
~~~
One of Daves rear kick panels (under the back seat, in front of the battery)was buckled, so he decided to replace both of them.  Using the good one as a template, Dave cut two new panels out of masonite.  He first tried to cover them with black naugahyde that he purchased at a local fabric store.  He tried to glue the naugahyde to the masonite with contact cement -- a miserable failure, as the contact cement made a real mess of the naugahyde.  So Dave cut a second pair and just painted them black.
